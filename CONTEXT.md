# Cool Spot

Cool Spot helps people remember locations and discover reviewed places where they can cool down in London. Private location saving is kept distinct from public cooling information and community reports.

## Language

**Location Anchor**:
A neutral reference to a real-world location. It may identify a recognised place, an exact position within a larger place, or a coordinate that has not yet been matched to a place.
_Avoid_: Cool Spot when cooling status is not yet known

**Saved Location**:
A private location a person saves for later. Saving it does not claim that the location is cool and does not create a public contribution.
_Avoid_: Remembered Spot, Cool Spot

**Cool Spot**:
A publicly visible location with reviewed cooling information. It may be based on a recognised place or a specific position within one.
_Avoid_: Saved Location, pin

**Contribution**:
Information a person prepares or submits to create or update a Cool Spot. A Contribution is not public until it passes review.
_Avoid_: Saved Location

**Visit Report**:
A person's time-specific account of their experience at an existing Cool Spot. It does not replace the Cool Spot's stable place information.
_Avoid_: review, rating
