# Cool Spot

Cool Spot helps people remember locations and discover reviewed places where they can cool down in London. Private location saving is kept distinct from public cooling information and community reports.

## Language

**Location Anchor**:
A neutral reference to a real-world location. It may identify a recognised place, an exact position within a larger place, or a coordinate that has not yet been matched to a place.
_Avoid_: Cool Spot when cooling status is not yet known

**Saved Location**:
A private coordinate a person saves so they can recognise or return to it later. Saving it does not imply an intention to contribute, confirm a visit, claim that the location is cool, or create public information.
_Avoid_: Remembered Spot, Cool Spot

**Recognised Place**:
A named real-world place known to a map or official data source. It is not a Cool Spot unless reviewed cooling information has been published for it.
_Avoid_: Cool Spot when no cooling information exists

**Cool Spot**:
A publicly visible location with reviewed cooling information. It may be based on a recognised place or a specific position within one.
_Avoid_: Saved Location, pin

**Contribution**:
Information a person prepares or submits to create or update a Cool Spot. A Contribution is not public until it passes review.
_Avoid_: Saved Location

**Place Information**:
Reviewed facts about what a Cool Spot offers, such as cooling features, access, seating, opening information and a place photo. It is distinct from a person's time-specific experience and may be corrected when circumstances change.
_Avoid_: long-term data, permanent information

**Visit Report**:
A person's time-specific account of how an existing Cool Spot felt relative to conditions outside. It is distinct from Live Presence and does not replace the Cool Spot's Place Information.
_Avoid_: review, rating

**Live Presence**:
A proximity-checked, anonymous indication that a person is currently using a Cool Spot to cool down. It expires after ten minutes and describes current use, not cooling effectiveness, capacity or seat availability.
_Avoid_: availability, current conditions, verified cool

**Visit Confirmation**:
A private, time-limited record that a device was near a specific Cool Spot when its user deliberately shared Live Presence or began a Visit Report. It can permit the Visit Report to be completed later, but does not prove that the person entered the place.
_Avoid_: verified visit, location history
