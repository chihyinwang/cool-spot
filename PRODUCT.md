# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

ios

## Purpose and scope

Cool Spot helps people discover places in London where they could cool down, understand the available evidence, and decide whether to go. Private saving, reviewed place information, a visitor's experience and current use are different signals.

This document records current implemented behavior and agreed product intent. Implementation is not proof of usability. The source baseline and working instructions are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); verification status and owner tests are in [OWNER-JOURNEY-WALKTHROUGH.md](OWNER-JOURNEY-WALKTHROUGH.md).

## Users

People exposed to heat while outside or travelling, potentially tired, hurried, using one hand or working with limited attention. The primary design target is a useful decision within 30 seconds, including deciding not to go when information is insufficient. This is a target, not a measured result. Saving, reporting and contributing are secondary tasks.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, fresh and helpful. Preserve the native SwiftUI interface, system typography, SF Symbols and teal/mint identity while improving clarity. Mutual aid should help someone else, not turn heat exposure into competitive engagement.

## References and anti-references

Apple Maps and iOS conventions inform familiar map/navigation behavior; Google Maps informs separation of saving, visitor experience and place corrections. These are design references, not claims that our specific flows are platform requirements. Avoid tourism-style rewards, leaderboards, database jargon, decorative dashboard patterns and unsupported claims that a place is currently cool, safe, open or has seats available.

## Design Principles

1. Prioritise the decision to visit and Directions over invitations to contribute.
2. Distinguish known facts, missing information, example data and a person's report; never infer individual reports from aggregate totals.
3. Make private versus public consequences clear before commitment. Minimise required answers and unnecessary navigation.
4. Group related questions, use concrete language and keep labels visible while typing. Do not expose internal place-resolution categories to users.
5. Follow native navigation, controls and accessibility. Target AA contrast, 44-point touch areas, Dynamic Type, dark appearance, Reduce Motion and meaningful VoiceOver order. These are requirements, not claims of a completed accessibility audit.

## Shared language

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Location anchor | Reference to a recognised place, a point inside a larger place, or an unmatched coordinate; no cooling claim by itself. |
| Recognised place | A named map/source place. It becomes a Cool Spot only when reviewed cooling information is published. Sources include live Apple Maps search results and retained prototype fixtures. |
| Cool Spot | A public location record with published cooling information, either imported from an identified cooling-space source or published after contribution review. It may describe a whole venue or a specific cooling area within one. Normal launches show 250 GLA 2025 records and three labelled examples (Tate Modern and two fictional places). This name does not guarantee current cooling, opening or availability. Ordinary map results, private Pins and pending proposals are not Cool Spots. Older examples remain accessible through existing saved/report records and explicit example mode. |
| Saved place / private Pin | A private bookmark or coordinate. Neither confirms a visit nor initiates a contribution. `SavedLocation` is the code model for both; the UI separates Places and Pins. |
| Place information / contribution | Facts such as cooling features, eligibility, cost and seating, or a proposal to add/correct them. A proposal awaits review; it is not a visitor's time-specific report. |
| Visit Report | Experience relative to outside at a stated visit time. It does not replace place information or automatically share presence. |
| Live Presence | Anonymous indication of current use for ten minutes after a deliberate nearby action. Not seat availability, all occupants or cooling effectiveness. |
| Visit Confirmation | Private per-place record created by a deliberate nearby report/presence action. It supports later reporting but does not prove entry into the venue. Proximity is simulated. |

## Discovery and private saving

Sources: `ExploreView` in [ExploreView.swift](cool-spot/ExploreView.swift), `SavedView`/`SavedPlaceEditor` in [SavedYouViews.swift](cool-spot/SavedYouViews.swift), and `PrototypeStore` in [PrototypeModels.swift](cool-spot/PrototypeModels.swift).

- Explore, Saved and You are the three tabs. Explore search and the contribution chooser use native `MKLocalSearch` for place names, landmarks, addresses and postcodes, alongside the bundled Cool Spot list. The Tate Modern, Example Community Room and Example Shaded Garden comparison examples are included in normal search/map discovery; other older examples remain in explicit example mode or retained saved records. The existing result → place detail / contribution form routes remain. Ordinary Apple Maps results have no reviewed cooling information, Report or Cooling here actions.
- `PlaceSearchModel` in [PlaceSearch.swift](cool-spot/PlaceSearch.swift) waits 350 ms after typing; Search and Try again submit immediately. Empty input cancels work. Replaced requests are cancelled and late results ignored. Loading, no matches and failure with retry are distinct; a failed request does not hide matching Cool Spot list entries or saved places. Results scroll instead of being limited to two ordinary places. While a nonempty search field is focused, the nearby strip is temporarily hidden to make room above the keyboard; it returns on Search, loss of focus or clearing.
- Explore provides the visible map region as a search hint. The contribution chooser uses its original source location (including a saved Pin or selected place). A region is a hint, not a London-only boundary. Apple may return approximate matches for nonsense input; absence of an exact text match is not an API failure. No-match feedback keeps the existing add-location entry. Search requires network access, but does not request GPS permission.
- Filters are single-select: Indoor, Outdoor shade, AC, Free and Water. More is not implemented. Search this area only resets its UI state; it does not fetch another region. Nearby and location explanations are simulated.
- Save bookmarks a place under Saved → Places. Selected Apple Maps results use their Place ID, or a deterministic name/coordinate fallback if unavailable. Saved results retain name, address, coordinates and known type locally so the same detail can reopen after relaunch; existing v1 journeys remain readable. Search alone does not save a bookmark, confirm a visit or publish a place.
- MapKit categories supply a place type only where mapped; unknown types stay optional. Search never infers an indoor/outdoor setting or cooling facilities. Ordinary place details lead with name, source category when mapped, address and cooling-information status; no decorative cover is shown. Direct Call and Website actions are omitted from all app-controlled place cards; supplementary contact information is available through Place details when the system card supplies it. Existing saved contact metadata remains compatible. The broad Cool Spot place type remains separate from the more specific source category; unknown categories are not guessed.
- Save a pin here saves the fixed simulated current coordinate under Pins, not the map camera centre. Pins can have private names/notes; there is no Pin-delete UI.
- Both kinds of saved place open the same respective detail view used from Explore. **Save** immediately bookmarks the place; **Saved** opens a menu with **Edit saved place** and **Remove from Saved**. Editing opens `SavedPlaceEditor` with Name in Saved and Your note; **Save changes** is enabled only after a change and with a nonblank name. Cancel protects unsaved edits; the editor does not autosave. The place card shows a read-only **Your note** section only when a note exists, with Edit. There is no inline private-details form added by saving. Pins use the same editor through **Edit saved pin**. Removing a place with a note requires confirmation; visit reports are retained. Preparing a public contribution does not copy private text or move a saved Pin.
- Selecting a map marker, nearby Cool Spot or search result focuses the map on its coordinates. An ordinary selected place gets a marker without becoming a Cool Spot. Explore details start at a native medium sheet and expand by dragging upward. The visible map stays above the sheet; Directions and Save remain at the card bottom. Cool Spot cards lead with one cooling-features block (two priority features, with expansion in place), cost/seating and known entry information. **Visitor reports** groups the experience summary, latest-visit date, always-visible distribution, optional stay-length disclosure, latest individual report preview, the single **Read all reports** navigation link and the report-entry action. The preview itself is read-only; there is no duplicate View all link beside the statistics. Places without individual reports show **No reports yet** without a reading link. The aggregate summary is absent when there is no evidence, and stay-length disclosure is absent when there are no stay reports. Available published photos form a compact strip after cooling/access facts and before facilities; absent photos create no placeholder block. Example place data is labelled as such, without claiming a real authority import.
- “Seating provided” describes the fact that a place has seating, not current vacant seats. Display wording is updated in cards and contribution choices; the stored enum value is unchanged for compatibility.
- Ordinary places offer **Find nearby Cool Spots** before invitations to contribute. Explore switches to results around that selected place, initially within 1 km; **Search a wider area** expands to 3, 6, 12, 24 and 25 km. Only the existing Cool Spot list is searched, ordered by straight-line distance from the selected place; the result card uses the same distance context. A missing result does not claim there are no cooling opportunities. Closing a result returns to the same radius/results; **Back to [place]** restores the original card and the Explore search text. From Saved, the same action opens Explore around that saved place. This prototype Cool Spot list contains all 250 GLA 2025 records and three labelled community cooling examples; it is a dated snapshot, not a live London service. Report/presence eligibility is unchanged.
- `ApplePlaceInformationView` in PlaceDetailView puts **Place details** on all places with a known map identity, before a collapsed **View nearby streets** disclosure. The disclosure requests native Look Around only when expanded. Recognised Apple places with a valid Place ID resolve the exact map item and pass it to `MKLookAroundSceneRequest(mapItem:)`; the system place card resolves the same Place ID on demand. Place details and the collapsed nearby-streets disclosure sit together in the first place-information section, before visitor evidence. A regular-weight Suggest an edit action ends that section on Cool Spot cards. Coordinate-only results and fixtures use a coordinate request without inventing an Apple business identity. Apple chooses the street scene, which may not show the entrance or indoor conditions. The control and compact caption call it nearby streets, not an entrance photo. Loading, unavailable coverage and retryable failure have distinct text. Street views around fixture Cool Spots are labelled as near an example location. Closing the system card returns to the cooling card. This supplementary information makes no cooling claim.
- Directions on both kinds of place detail open an external Apple Maps walking link with the selected coordinates. Navigation itself is not implemented or verified inside this app.

## Cool Spots and three-state comparison prototype

Sources: [CoolSpots.prototype.json](cool-spot/Resources/CoolSpots.prototype.json), `CoolSpotsResponse` in [CoolSpotsResponse.swift](cool-spot/CoolSpotsResponse.swift), and `existingSpot(for:)` / `searchCoolSpots(query:including:)` in PrototypeModels.

- Normal launches decode a bundled API-shaped response (`schemaVersion: 4`, `datasetID`, `items`) containing all **250** records in the [GLA Cool Space Data 2025](https://data.london.gov.uk/dataset/cool-space-data-2025-2z19p) snapshot. The reader also accepts legacy v1/v2/v3. The raw [GeoJSON](data/cool-spots/gla-cool-spaces-2025.geojson) and SHA-256 are retained; its historical retrieval time is unknown in the hash-bound manifest. `runtime` remains raw source metadata, never a verification date. Source names and free-text facts are whitespace-normalised; known facility codes and the source ventilation typo are mapped explicitly. Broad place types use documented name rules or remain unknown; they are not claimed to be source classifications. **GLA · 2025** identifies the dated snapshot in the UI.
- Each record has a fixed internal UUID in the [identity registry](data/cool-spots/identity-registry.json), separate from source record IDs and Apple Place IDs. The ten existing UUIDs are preserved. New UUIDs are deterministically seeded for this dataset; future annual source IDs must be reconciled against the registry, not presumed stable. Bookmarks, reports and notes continue to reference our identity. All source records can appear using their GLA coordinates even without an Apple match.
- The offline [candidate collector](scripts/cool_spots/MatchApplePlaces.swift) runs serial `MKLocalSearch` requests and checkpoints candidate IDs, names, coordinates, distances and addresses. Address queries and the bounded [discovery probe](scripts/cool_spots/AuditDiscovery.swift) supplement initial results; adding more words can hide valid candidates, so the probe also records the unmodified place name with the app's request types. The [adapter](scripts/cool_spots/build_cool_spots.py) accepts one candidate with a Place ID, exact or >=0.94 normalised name similarity, agreeing street or postcode, no conflicting street number/postcode/category and distance <=150 m. Numeric address line 2 participates in number checks. Street/St is expanded only within addresses; public-transport, parking and restroom results cannot automatically become a cooling venue. These are conservative heuristics, not probability scores or human approval. Source-reviewed exceptions retain official evidence, source fingerprints and candidate snapshots in [review decisions](data/cool-spots/review-decisions.json); changed snapshots invalidate prior decisions. Current totals: 136 automatic same-place links, 7 source-reviewed same-place links, 1 reviewed containing-venue link, 87 candidate reviews and 19 no-candidate records. Source review is by Codex, not owner acceptance. Exact accepted same-place IDs alone drive runtime merging. A reviewed within_place link can surface the cooling area when searching its containing venue and open that venue's Place details, but never merges identities or extends cooling facts to the entire building. Unresolved records may appear separately from Apple results; no fuzzy merge runs in the user's search flow.
- The [complete mapping export](data/cool-spots/cool-spots-mapping.json) includes every converted record, candidates, queries, evidence, conversion warnings and review reasons. The compact app response excludes candidate/audit payloads. `no_candidate` means these queries found no candidate, not that the venue cannot exist in Apple Maps; genuine service failures remain separate. Review must establish same place versus a room/venue relationship before publishing an ID. The [ten-case discovery audit](data/cool-spots/discovery-audit.json) records bounded API/native evidence and remaining issues; it is not verification of all 250 places or current cooling conditions. A containing building is not silently treated as the cooling area.
- JSON uses numeric coordinates, stable string codes decoded to enums, explicit unknown values and nullable text/hours. Additional fields are ignored by the reader, unknown codes degrade to unknown, and unsupported major versions/invalid identities fail visibly. The [JSON Schema](data/cool-spots/cool-spots-response.schema.json) validates the v4 producer response. This supports additive evolution; renamed fields, changed meanings or required new fields need a version/migration decision.
- The response is a read model, not a database layout or public write request. The adapter continues to use existing SwiftUI/domain flows. Replacing the bundled reader with a backend fetch can preserve this shape; GLA API/snapshot ingestion and Apple identity reconciliation belong behind that boundary. The source registry requires only ID/provider/label; dataset/download/hash fields are optional import metadata, so community sources do not need fictitious GLA-style metadata. The adapter maps community sources as community and unknown providers as an unclassified Cool Spot, never an official GLA venue. No live Cool Spot list backend, automatic refresh or remote moderation/upload service was introduced.
- A shared `PlaceInformation` value supplies facts independently of GLA/community source. Both detail types use `PlaceIdentityHeader` for name → address → category and optional compact source. Cool Spot cards show cooling features, then known cost/seating, eligibility, specific cooling area and posted stay limit directly. Supplemental information is grouped immediately below: **Facilities & accessibility** expands known toilets, wheelchair access, staff-on-site and tables facts. It starts collapsed and appears only when data exists, including known negative values. Cooling-space hours are no longer shown on the app-controlled place card; source hours remain in the Cool Spot list data. A wheelchair-only record still has the facilities disclosure. Known drinking water and seating remain in the summary without duplication; an explicit negative drinking-water fact is shown inside facilities. **Place details** follows the optional facilities row in the same group, before Visitor reports. Missing data creates no empty rows; B/C retain the same Place details label and relative position after their cooling summary. Source attribution appears once beside the category, with a link when supplied. Hours remain source text with a timezone; the app does not calculate Open now or equate these hours with Apple business hours. The system card is supplementary and does not promise opening hours exist for every place. No photo, visitor report or presence count is fabricated for a GLA record; synthetic community examples remain labelled and separate.
- The bundled `access.staffedWhenOpen` is optional and mapped from GLA `is_staffed_when_open` from the same source record; missing, unknown and future codes remain unknown. Maximum seating and the 2025 temperature description are not presented as live availability or temperature. GLA target-group metadata is not converted into admission restrictions. Eligibility is unknown when the source has no per-place access statement; a general dataset description is not treated as proof for every venue. Reports retain their individual visit times; they do not overwrite these place facts. No automated conflict-resolution or moderation backend is implemented.
- GLA Cool Spot list places start with no visitor reports or nearby confirmation. Labelled community examples have synthetic visitor evidence but do not create personal confirmations. Existing report/presence eligibility is retained; **You → Settings → Prototype controls** can simulate being near a chosen place on a QA device. This does not claim GPS detection or a public report service.
- Three cases are available in one normal launch: **Canning Town Library** (GLA cooling information), **Tate Modern** (non-GLA community-style cooling example, with a real checked Apple ID), and **The British Museum** (ordinary place, no cooling record in this Cool Spot list). Tate Modern is labelled **Example cooling info** in the card, search and discovery strip; its cooling, entry and facilities facts and three visitor reports are illustrative. Example Community Room and Example Shaded Garden are fictional places with two reports each and different known/unknown facilities. All three show Example cooling info; individual reports show Example visitor report. Their aggregate distributions and latest visit dates derive from those same fixed-date records. There are no fake personal submissions, confirmations, bookmarks or map identities. Only Tate Modern has a checked Apple Place ID; fictional places do not open an unrelated system place card. Its independent internal ID also supports normal save/report flows. The place facts now use `Resources/CommunityCoolSpots.prototype.json` and the same v4 decoder as GLA; `PrototypeComparisonPlaces` only attaches the separately modelled visitor reports and simulated presence counts.
- The British Museum has a minimal remembered map identity on normal launch so its ordinary-place card can be found even before live search completes. Fresh MapKit results take precedence over this seed, including available contact metadata. It is never inserted into the Cool Spot list or treated as proof of no cooling facilities at the actual venue. A/B share the same card; C keeps its no-information/nearby-discovery state. Directions, Save, Place details and nearby-street controls keep the same names across the states.
- Normal Cool Spot list launches preserve existing saved items, notes and reports, including old examples. A saved Apple identity with a known Cool Spot list match opens the same Cool Spot and keeps its existing bookmark/note. Explicit `--example-cool-spots`, shape previews and report examples use memory-only fixture stores; they do not establish persistence evidence.

## Cool Spots contract and published photos

The v4 producer schema and adapter are implemented (v1/v2/v3 reads remain compatible); the future service boundaries below are design decisions, not claims of a running API. API choices follow [Microsoft resource-oriented API design](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/best-practices/api-design), [Google resource identity](https://google.aip.dev/122), [compatibility](https://google.aip.dev/180) and [enum evolution](https://google.aip.dev/126). We adopt stable identity and compatibility principles, not Google's exact field naming conventions.

The owner-requested naming change on 2026-09-25 separates the resource from its transport: `CoolSpot` is one domain record; `CoolSpotsResponse` is the complete list response, and `coolSpotsResponse` is its TypeScript value. `items` contains the individual public records, while `sources` describes their origins. The current handler is `createListCoolSpotsHandler`; its generic read failure code is `cool_spots_load_failed`. `datasetID` identifies the dataset, not a place, response page or HTTP request; each place keeps its own `items[].id`. Renaming this wire key makes the producer contract v4. Swift continues to decode the previous key for versions 1–3. Existing local publications are read through a legacy storage-key alias and saved under `publishedCoolSpotRecords`; place IDs and other journey data are preserved.

This naming leaves room for distinct operations: a list at `GET /cool-spots`, one record at `GET /cool-spots/{id}`, reports associated with that record at `/cool-spots/{id}/reports`, and reviewable proposals at `/place-contributions`. These paths illustrate resource boundaries; routing, pagination and write contracts are still to be implemented and agreed in their own slices. A mixed search across ordinary places and Cool Spots must preserve the distinction between result types. Separate list/detail or write payloads can evolve without renaming the Cool Spot resource.

| Field group | Type and purpose | Ownership / display |
|---|---|---|
| `id`, `name`, `location`, `address` | Opaque service ID (new records use UUIDs); text; numeric coordinates with venue/specific-area/unknown scope; structured address with nullable parts and optional formatted address | Identity is service-owned. Name/address appear consistently in the header. Do not substitute Apple/GLA IDs for our UUID. |
| `placeType`, `setting`, `coolingFeatures`, `coolingDetails` | Stable string codes; feature array; nullable extra text | Known cooling facts form the main summary. Water nearby has its own water_nearby code; drinking water remains an access fact. Other and unknown place types are distinct. Unsupported codes remain unknown. A source import can have no recognised feature without manufacturing one. |
| `access` | Cost/eligibility codes; yes/limited/no/unknown seating; yes/no/unknown water, wheelchair, staff and tables; toilets on_site/nearby/not_on_site/none/unknown; nullable eligibility detail and areaDescription; postedStayLimit with limited + positive minutes, no_stated_limit, or unknown | Summary shows cost/seating/entry; facilities disclosure shows known supplemental facilities. No live vacancy claim. Unknown is not false. Area identification is not crowd-sourced navigation. |
| `hours` | Nullable original text + IANA timezone | Retained source information, currently hidden at owner request. Not inferred from contributions or used to calculate Open now. |
| `photos` | Ordered array of published photo resources; empty array means no published photos | Photos B: up to three 108 pt thumbnails under summary, then optional See all → gallery → full-screen viewer. Tapping a thumbnail opens that exact photo. Caption/attribution and known capture date stay with the photo. No photo block for empty arrays. |
| `sourceReferences`, `provenance` | Ordered source/record references (primary source first) and field-level JSON Pointers, source record IDs, recording timestamps and import/derivation methods | Keep imported facts distinguishable from reviewed community edits. Unknown facts carry no fabricated evidence. Retrieval time is not verification time. Conflicting reviewed edits become Action needed instead of overwriting current facts. Each publication replaces evidence only for the edited fields. Locally edited GLA records retain their GLA identity and show a compact Local edits suffix, so new answers are not presented as an unchanged GLA import. |
| `mapReferences` | Accepted provider Place ID + same_place/within_place relationship + verification method + nullable checked time | Used to unify discovery/bookmarks, not shown as technical labels to app users. An explicitly identified specific area can use its parent venue for Place details without becoming a search/bookmark identity alias. Merely filling optional area text does not turn a selected venue into a separate place. Candidate scores/review notes are retained only in the audit. |

A photo resource has a stable `id`, `thumbnailURL`, `imageURL`, numeric `width`/`height`, optional `caption`, optional `capturedAt`/`publishedAt`, `attribution`, `source` and optional `contributionID`. Upload bytes, private uploader identity, EXIF/location metadata and pending/rejected moderation state do not belong in this public payload. Production photos should use HTTPS delivery; `bundle://` is only a labelled fixture transport; `prototype-photo://` refers to device-local files in this prototype and must be replaced by HTTPS delivery in a real service. Capture time must not be replaced with upload time. There is no public API guarantee that a URL itself remains the photo identity.

Place-information contributions (including submitted photos), visitor experiences, ephemeral presence and private saved notes have independent lifecycles. Future writes should use separate contribution/report resources, upload photo bytes separately, and let the server assign IDs, moderation outcomes, provenance and accepted map links. Public list/detail responses return approved facts and photos only; clients must not submit an entire read response as an update. A network Cool Spot list should add cursor pagination and bounded photo previews with a separate photo-list route once needed; do not embed unbounded report/photo history into every map result. These endpoints are not implemented here.

The selected photo remains pending until an explicit **Publish locally** simulation. Submission persists private image files separately from JSON/preferences; publication adds a sanitized, resized image and thumbnail reference to the public record. Capture date remains unknown. This is device-local and does not upload anything or expose the photo to other devices. The owner can try three generated, explicitly illustrative photographs on **Example Community Room** (entrance, seating, drinking water); they do not depict a real venue. Existing Riverside Library example imagery is retained as one photo. GLA records and real Tate Modern receive no invented photography. Remote HTTPS loading and retry rendering exist, but no production media service has been exercised.

The contribution adapter writes only changed fields for updates. Unanswered questions do not erase source facts; unchecking Drinking water withdraws that assertion to unknown, never no. The general public note becomes `additionalInformation`, shown in a secondary **More information** disclosure. Correction/removal reasons stay private in the proposal. `areaDescription` replaces `instructions`, and the structured stay limit replaces `postedStayLimitMinutes`; old names remain readable but are not emitted. Reserved eligibilityDetails is not a new form question.

GLA records use unknown scope and eligibility when the source lacks per-record evidence. Dataset context still supplies indoors, London, GB and Europe/London; name-based place classification remains explicitly inferred. Source download metadata is hash-bound in `data/cool-spots/source-manifest.json`; the historical retrieval timestamp is unknown, not reconstructed from filesystem modification time. A future source download must record its actual UTC retrieval time with its hash. Source refresh/conflict review and a real contribution write API remain separate future work.

## Reading hierarchy and interface copy

- Related metadata uses 4 pt spacing, labels and fields 8 pt, related items 12–16 pt and sections 24 pt, with 20 pt page insets where a native Form/List does not own the layout. Place-card major groups use native dividers with 16 pt spacing on each side; related content within a group uses whitespace. These roles are shared as `LayoutSpacing` in PrototypeSharedViews. Native forms and lists retain platform grouping. Touch controls keep at least a 44 pt target.
- Place-card information actions use leading SF Symbols: list.bullet for Facilities & accessibility, info.circle for Place details, binoculars for View nearby streets and text.bubble for Read all reports. Suggest an edit uses square.and.pencil and regular-weight text without a trailing chevron. They share a scalable 20 pt icon column and 8 pt text gap, retaining text labels and existing chevrons; section headings remain text-only.
- The first place-card section groups identity, cooling/access facts, facilities, supplementary place/street information and the correction entry. Visitor reports is a separate second section. Current use is a third section containing the presence summary and sharing actions on one plain surface, without an inner colored card. Nonempty private notes follow as a separate section. Photos belong to the first place-information section, immediately after cooling/access facts; absent content produces no empty section or divider. Ordinary places use the same first-section ordering and divider treatment before Add cooling information and any private note. A heading sits closer to the content it introduces than to the preceding group. Do not compensate for unclear grouping by adding explanatory paragraphs.
- Remove repetitive interpretation disclaimers and prototype implementation narration from the reading path. Retain dates, actual missing-data/error states, compact Example/Demo labels and information needed before an action (privacy, duration, required input, losing edits). Longer eligibility help remains behind Why can’t I share? or How this works. No live-data or service claim is added by shortening copy.

## Visitor reports and presence

Sources: `PrototypeStore.canReportVisit`, `hasCurrentConfirmation`, `beginVisitReport`, `beginNewVisitReport`, `discardReportAnswers`, `submitReport`, `checkIn` and `visitorReportItems` in [PrototypeModels.swift](cool-spot/PrototypeModels.swift); `VisitReportFlow` in [PlaceDetailView.swift](cool-spot/PlaceDetailView.swift); [VisitorReportsView.swift](cool-spot/VisitorReportsView.swift).

- A report can start while nearby or from a previously confirmed, unreported visit. Confirmed visits have no deadline to start or finish. Browsing and saving do not create confirmation; someone now away with no earlier confirmation cannot report through the current flow.
- The only required questionnaire answer is Not cooler / A little cooler / Much cooler. Visit time is prefilled, editable and cannot be in the future. Helping features, stay and comment are optional and remain on the form.
- Answers save as they change; Finish later, leaving and relaunch preserve them and the original visit time. You → Your reports separates Unfinished, Published and Visits you can report. Presence without answers is not an unfinished report.
- Discard answers clears only that draft, retaining confirmation. Publishing completes only the matching visit and rejects duplicate publication. A nearby user can explicitly Share a new visit after publishing; it preserves older reports. There is no edit/delete UI for published reports or multiple simultaneous drafts at the same place.
- Visitor reports and the single place-page preview use the same newest-visit-time-first list, regardless of author. Own and example reports share a renderer. Examples have labelled synthetic answers and fixed dates; missing optional fields are omitted. The three normal-launch community examples derive counts from their individual records; older legacy fixture aggregates can exceed their available individual records. The generic Before you go block is absent; Suggest an edit is secondary to reading.

I’m cooling off here is a separate nearby action. It adds one anonymous count for ten minutes, with one active place per device. Stop sharing removes that count; it does not revoke report eligibility. Relaunch preserves the original end time. Map number badges appear only above zero. GLA records and Tate Modern start at zero; the explicitly fictional Example Community Room and Example Shaded Garden carry fixed demo baselines of two and one people. These baselines are not live remote users and personal sharing adds one for its normal ten-minute duration. Nearby eligibility still uses Prototype controls rather than GPS; example visitor reports never grant the current user a visit confirmation. The How this works map animation illustrates 2 → 3 with Pause/Resume; Reduce Motion shows a static completed example and never shares real presence.
